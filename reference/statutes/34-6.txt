# 34 O.S. § 6 — Verification of Signatures
# Source: https://www.oscn.net/applications/oscn/DeliverDocument.asp?CiteID=71557
# Retrieved: 2026-09-01 (OSCN 2026 edition). Verbatim extract; blanks/underscores preserved.

Cite as: 34 O.S. § 6 (OSCN 2026), General Provisions
Any person who circulates a sheet of the petition shall be a registered voter of this state and shall verify the signatures included on any signature sheets he or she circulates by executing his or her affidavit thereon and as a part thereof. The Secretary of State shall cause to be affixed onto the back of the signature form an affidavit, subject to change, for the circulator to verify the signatures.
