# 34 O.S. § 24 — Only Substantial Compliance Required
# Source: https://www.oscn.net/applications/oscn/DeliverDocument.asp?CiteID=71575
# Retrieved: 2026-09-01 (OSCN 2026 edition). Verbatim extract; blanks/underscores preserved.

Cite as: 34 O.S. § 24 (OSCN 2026), General Provisions
The procedure herein prescribed is not mandatory, but 
if substantially followed will be sufficient. If the end aimed at can be 
attained and procedure shall be sustained, clerical and mere technical errors 
shall be disregarded.
