**PETITION FOR REFERENDUM**

**To the Honorable Ross Selman, Chairman of the Board of County Commissioners of Pittsburg County, Oklahoma:**

We, the undersigned legal voters of Pittsburg County, Oklahoma, respectfully order that the resolution adopted by the Board of County Commissioners of Pittsburg County, Oklahoma, on June 22, 2026, approving the Emerald ProjectCo Data Center Economic Development Project Plan and establishing Tax Abatement District No. 1 and Tax Abatement District No. 2, together with the Project Plan and any exhibits or agreements adopted as part of that resolution, be referred to the registered voters of Pittsburg County for their approval or rejection at an election as provided by law.

The measure to be referred concerns tax relief, tax incentives, tax exemptions, captured increments, or related public financing authorized under the Oklahoma Local Development Act for the development and construction of a data center project in Pittsburg County.

This referendum petition is brought pursuant to the initiative and referendum powers reserved to the people with reference to tax relief, incentives, exemptions, and captured increments authorized by the Oklahoma Local Development Act.
