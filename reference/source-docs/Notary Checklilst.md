

| NOTARY CHECKLIST Petition circulator affidavits \- Use before leaving the notary table |
| :---: |

| Goal: protect every signature sheet from being rejected because of a bad or incomplete circulator affidavit. |  |
| ----- | ----- |
|  **BEFORE NOTARIZING** **\[ \]** The full petition pamphlet is still intact and pages are not detached. **\[ \]** The affidavit is for the signature sheet immediately before it or otherwise clearly identified. **\[ \]** The circulator personally witnessed every signature on that sheet. **\[ \]** The circulator is present in front of the notary. **\[ \]** The circulator has not signed the affidavit before appearing before the notary, unless the notary can lawfully handle the acknowledgment under Oklahoma rules. **\[ \]** The pamphlet number and sheet number are recorded in the tracking log. |  **AFFIDAVIT MUST SHOW** **\[ \]** State of Oklahoma and correct county in the venue line. **\[ \]** Circulator printed name. **\[ \]** Circulator signature. **\[ \]** Circulator complete address, if the affidavit requests it. **\[ \]** Date the affidavit is sworn or acknowledged. **\[ \]** Notary signature. **\[ \]** Notary printed name or title. **\[ \]** Notary commission number. **\[ \]** Notary commission expiration date. **\[ \]** Official Oklahoma notary seal. |

|  BEFORE LEAVING THE NOTARY \[ \] Check that the seal is visible and not cut off. \[ \] Check that the notary commission number is written clearly. \[ \] Check that the expiration date is complete and readable. \[ \] Check that the notary signed in the correct place. \[ \] Check that the circulator signed in the correct place. \[ \] Flag any questionable affidavit for Petition Captain review. |  DO NOT ACCEPT WITHOUT REVIEW \[ \] Missing notary seal. \[ \] Missing notary signature. \[ \] Missing circulator signature. \[ \] Missing commission number or expiration date. \[ \] Wrong county in the venue line without correction. \[ \] Affidavit detached from the signature sheet or packet. \[ \] Affidavit signed by someone other than the circulator. \[ \] Anything crossed out, altered, or unclear without explanation. |
| ----- | ----- |

Source notes: 34 O.S. § 6 requires petition circulators to verify signatures by affidavit. 34 O.S. § 3 treats the petition copy plus signature sheets as a petition pamphlet. 62 O.S. § 868 governs Local Development Act referendum filing before circulation. This checklist is operational guidance, not legal advice.
