**FRONT: Voter conversation, signer rules, and line check**

|  WHAT TO SAY • “We are collecting signatures to let county voters decide whether to approve or reject the proposed 85% data center tax abatement.” • “Signing does not force you to vote yes or no. It means you support putting the issue to a public vote.” • If asked: “This petition refers the county resolution to voters for approval or rejection.” • If they disagree: “Thank you for your time.” Move on calmly. |  WHO CAN SIGN • A registered voter in this county. • The signer must sign their own name. • The signer should use voter-registration information, not a temporary address. • One signature per person. No duplicates. • No one signs for a spouse, parent, neighbor, or friend. |
| ----- | ----- |
|  **EACH SIGNER FILLS OUT** **•** Signature **•** Legal first name **•** Legal last name **•** House number **•** Street name **•** City or town **•** ZIP code **•** Birth month and day in MM/DD format |  **BEFORE THEY WALK AWAY** **•** Check that every field is readable. **•** Check that birth date is month/day only, not full year. **•** Check that only one voter used the line. **•** Fix errors while signer is present: single line through error, write correction, signer initials if possible. **• Do not** fill missing information later. |
| **ABSOLUTE RULE: Use only official filed petition pamphlets. No loose sheets. No homemade forms. No signatures before filing.** |  |

Source notes: Based on Oklahoma referendum and petition requirements in 62 O.S. § 868 and 34 O.S. §§ 1, 3, and 6\. This quick card is operational guidance, not legal advice.

| CIRCULATOR QUICK CARD BACK: Packet protection, affidavit basics, and return rules |
| :---: |

|  DO NOT • Do not collect signatures before the Petition Captain says the filed packet is ready. • Do not detach, reorder, replace, or remove pages. • Do not leave the packet unattended. • Do not let voters take the packet away from you. • Do not fill in missing voter info after the signer leaves. • Do not argue, pressure, threaten, or mislead anyone. |  PACKET RULES • Each pamphlet is a complete packet and must stay together. • Track the pamphlet number and your name in the log. • Keep completed sheets clean and dry. • Do not photograph or post completed signature lines online. • Return packets daily or when instructed by the Petition Captain. |
| ----- | ----- |
|  **AFFIDAVIT BASICS** **•** You must personally witness every signature on sheets you circulate. **•** You sign only your own circulator affidavit. **•** Do not sign a blank affidavit. **•** Do not sign for another circulator. **•** Have the affidavit notarized after signatures are collected. **•** Make sure the affidavit matches the signature sheet it verifies. |  **IF CHALLENGED OR HARASSED** **•** Stay calm. Do not argue. **•** Say: “Signing only supports putting the issue to a public vote.” **•** Step away if anyone becomes aggressive. **•** Call the Petition Captain immediately. **•** Write down date, time, location, witnesses, and what happened. **•** Return the packet if it may have been damaged or compromised. |
