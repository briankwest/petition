When it actually started The IREN / Emerald ProjectCo data center, Pittsburg County, Oklahoma · the dated record from the first deposit to the tabled vote

The public heard about this project at two hearings in June 2026. The paper says it began a year earlier. IREN had money on deposit for an Oklahoma expansion by the end of June 2025; a battery-storage company was asking McAlester for six million gallons of water a day in July; the county’s review committee met five times before a resident attended. This page lays the dates out in order, each with its source, and says plainly where the record goes quiet.

01 / The short version

Eight lines

every line carries its source
  1. IREN’s balance sheet at 30 June 2025 already carried security deposits “for expansion projects in British Columbia, Canada, Oklahoma, USA and West Texas, USA.” The company said so in November. Its annual report, filed in August, does not contain the word Oklahoma.12
  2. Emerald ProjectCo Inc. was incorporated in Delaware on 29 July 2025. The next day, 30 July, Black Mountain Energy Storage wrote to the City of McAlester asking for six million gallons of raw water a day to cool “the proposed IREN data center.” The council voted to negotiate on 12 August.678
  3. Between October and December 2025 IREN bought land purchase options and a 1,600 MW connection right from PSO in Oklahoma for $112.0 million, $105.1 million of it for the power. That is on its books before the county’s committee had met.35
  4. The county’s first public word is an agenda item posted on 7 November 2025: a resolution to appoint members to “the Iren Data Center TID-TIF Committee.” The Board postponed it three times, once because “the names are needed by December 1st,” and adopted it on 1 December.11121314
  5. The committee first met on 8 December 2025 with the county’s outside counsel on the phone and six IREN representatives in the room. Asked about attorney fees, IREN’s representative said “Iren would reimburse for the all expenses.” Emerald ProjectCo qualified to do business in Oklahoma eight days later.166
  6. On 17 December IREN told the committee this is “their first project outside of Texas.” On 3 February 2026 the land was “under option o purchase” and the ask was “85% abatement or reimbursement, whatever the taxing entities prefer.” On 2 March IREN had “closed on the 1st property.” IREN’s first public announcement of an Oklahoma campus came on 5 February, between those meetings.1719204
  7. The first meeting a member of the public attended was the sixth, on 7 April 2026. Asked whether any non-disclosure agreement was in place, PSO’s representative said “PSO may have one.” The motion to recommend the plan died for lack of a second; two weeks later it passed unanimously.2122
  8. The public hearings were on 8 and 22 June 2026. By then IREN had been on the ground for a year, the county’s counsel had been drafting for four months, and the state had passed a law requiring data-center developers to notify county commissioners within sixty days of buying land. It took effect on 1 July, after the land here had changed hands.242710

03 / Where the record goes quiet

What the county’s file does not show

each gap is a records request

These are absences in the posted record, not accusations. Each one is a document that should exist somewhere, and the Open Records Act is the way to ask for it.

  • Who first called whomNothing on the county’s posted record precedes the 7 November 2025 agenda. IREN had deposits down by June and a water intermediary in McAlester by July. The correspondence that brought the project to the courthouse is not on the file.1729
  • The engagement of counselDavid Floyd appears at the committee’s first meeting, and IREN’s representative says the company will reimburse the county’s expenses. No agenda item engaging Floyd & Driver, approving a fee, or accepting the reimbursement appears in the Board agendas read for this page, May 2025 to July 2026.1629
  • Who created the committeeThe resolutions on the record appoint members. The document that constituted the committee, set its name, and produced the “list of 7 recommended names” is not posted.1416
  • The questions and the answersCounsel’s written information request of December 2025 and the developer’s responses presented on 3 February 2026 are described in the minutes and published nowhere.1719
  • The draftsA draft Project Plan and a draft Economic Development Agreement were reviewed on 2 March 2026; the Tax Incentive Agreement, the document with the numbers, has never been posted in any version.2022
  • 27 January 2026A meeting was noticed. No minutes are posted.18
  • Non-disclosure agreementsAsked on 7 April, PSO’s representative said the utility “may have one.” IREN said in June it signs none with government bodies. Whether any county official or committee member signed one is not on the record either way.2125
  • The Childress tripFour committee members toured IREN’s Texas campus before the vote. Who arranged it, who paid, and what was shown is not recorded beyond Rhyne’s summary.22
  • The landThe county’s record says only that the developer “closed on the 1st property.” The acreage and price come from a reporter reading the property records. The deeds themselves, and the name on them, are in the County Clerk’s land records.2010
  • Executive sessionsFor the record, none. The Board’s executive sessions in the autumn of 2025 were all personnel matters under 25 O.S. § 307(B)(1). The project was not discussed behind closed doors, so far as the agendas show; it simply was not discussed in public either.29
