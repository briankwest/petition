Before you come

  1. You must be a registered voter living in {{ petition.county }} County. Check in two minutes.
  2. Know the address on your voter registration and your birth month and day — the sheet asks for them.
  3. Sign only your own name, once. That is the law (34 O.S. § 23).

Circulators, notaries, drivers and event helpers are all needed. Circulators must be registered Oklahoma voters, 18 or older, and are trained before carrying a pamphlet.
