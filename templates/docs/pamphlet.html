Petition for Referendum

To the Honorable {{ p.addressee.name }}, {{ p.addressee.title }}:

We, the undersigned legal voters of the State of Oklahoma and of the county of {{ p.county }}, respectfully order that Resolution No. {{ f.resolution_number }}, entitled {{ f.resolution_title }}, passed by the {{ p.governing_body }}, on {{ f.adoption_date }}, shall be referred to the people of {{ p.county }} County for their approval or rejection at the {{ p.election.type }} election to be held on {{ f.election_date }}, {{ attestation }}

Referendum petitions on a county measure shall be filed with the Secretary of the County Election Board not more than thirty (30) days after the passage or adoption of the resolution. The time for filing this petition expires on {{ f.filing_deadline }}.

The question we herewith submit to our fellow voters is: Shall the following resolution (local legislation) be approved?

This referendum petition is brought under the initiative and referendum powers reserved to the people of every county with reference to tax relief, incentives, exemptions or captured increments by 62 O.S. § 868 and Section 6C of Article X of the Oklahoma Constitution. The form follows 34 O.S. § 1. An exact copy of the measure follows, then the proponents of record, then the signature sheets.
