NOTARY CHECKLIST — Petition circulator affidavits Use before leaving the notary table. {{ p.county }} County Data Center Tax Abatement Referendum.
Goal: protect every signature sheet from being rejected because of a bad or incomplete circulator affidavit. One notary defect throws out every signature on that sheet (34 O.S. § 6.1(A)(7)).

Before notarizing

  • The full petition pamphlet is intact; no pages detached.
  • The affidavit is on the back of the signature sheet it verifies and names that sheet number.
  • The circulator is a registered Oklahoma voter, 18 or older, and personally witnessed every signature on that sheet.
  • The circulator is present in front of the notary and signs in the notary's presence (or acknowledges a prior signature only if the notary can lawfully do so).
  • The pamphlet number and sheet number are recorded in the tracking log.

Affidavit must show (34 O.S. § 6)

  • Venue: State of Oklahoma, County of {{ p.county }}.
  • Circulator printed name and signature.
  • Circulator complete residence address.
  • Date the affidavit is sworn (day, month, year).
  • Notary signature and printed name/title.
  • Notary complete address.
  • Notary commission number.
  • Notary commission expiration date.
  • Official Oklahoma notary public seal, fully visible.

Before leaving the notary

  • Seal is visible and not cut off or smeared.
  • Commission number written clearly.
  • Expiration date complete and readable — and not expired.
  • Notary signed in the correct place; circulator signed in the correct place.
  • Nothing crossed out or altered without explanation.
  • Flag any questionable affidavit for Petition Captain review.

Do not accept without review

  • Missing notary seal or notary signature.
  • Missing circulator signature.
  • Missing commission number or expiration date.
  • Wrong county in the venue line without correction.
  • Affidavit detached from its signature sheet or the pamphlet.
  • Affidavit signed by someone other than the circulator.
  • Affidavit notarized before the signatures were collected.

Defect codes — 34 O.S. § 6.1(A): signatures the county will not count (bold = whole sheet lost)
