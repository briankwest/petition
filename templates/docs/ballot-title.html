Word count: {{ p.ballot_title_word_count }} (62 O.S. § 868(D)(1) limit: 150). A “yes” vote is a vote in favor of the resolution; a “no” vote is a vote against it.

Filed separately from the petition. Under 62 O.S. § 868(D)(1) the ballot title may be filed with the Secretary of the County Election Board before circulation and must be submitted no later than the signed copies of the petition. It contains the gist of the proposition in plain language, may not exceed 150 words, may not argue for or against the measure, and must say clearly what a yes vote and a no vote do. The Secretary forwards it to the District Attorney, who has three (3) days to approve it or file a conforming title (§ 868(D)(2)); any qualified elector may appeal the wording to the district court within ten (10) days (§ 868(E)). It is not printed on the petition pamphlet (compare 34 O.S. § 9(B)).
