CIRCULATOR QUICK CARD — {{ p.county }} County Data Center Tax Abatement Referendum Print duplex on legal paper. Keep with your petition supplies. FRONT: voter conversation, signer rules, line check.

What to say

  • “We are collecting signatures to let county voters decide whether to approve or reject the proposed {{ f.abatement }} data center tax abatement.”
  • “Signing does not force you to vote yes or no. It means you support putting the issue to a public vote.”
  • If asked: “This petition refers the county resolution to voters for approval or rejection.”
  • If they disagree: “Thank you for your time.” Move on calmly.

Who can sign

  • A registered voter who lives in {{ p.county }} County (62 O.S. § 868; 34 O.S. § 23).
  • The signer must sign their own name — signing for anyone else is a felony.
  • Use the name and address on their Oklahoma voter registration, not a temporary address.
  • One signature per person. No duplicates.
  • Not sure they are registered? They can check at {{ p.links.voter_portal }} or call the Election Board, {{ eb.phone }}.

Each signer fills out one full line

  • Signature
  • ★ Legal first name
  • ★ Legal last name
  • ★ House number
  • Street name
  • City or town
  • ★ ZIP code
  • ★ Birth month and day, MM/DD — not the year

Before they walk away

  • Every field readable? Birth date is month/day only?
  • Only one voter on the line, and on the printed line?
  • Fix errors while the signer is present: single line through the error, write the correction, signer initials.
  • Never fill in missing information after the signer leaves.

CIRCULATOR QUICK CARD — BACK Packet protection, affidavit basics, why sheets get thrown out, return rules.

Do not

  • Collect signatures before the Petition Captain says the filed packet is ready.
  • Detach, reorder, replace or remove pages.
  • Leave the packet unattended or let a voter take it away.
  • Fill in missing voter information after the signer leaves.
  • Argue, pressure, threaten or mislead anyone.

Packet rules

  • Each pamphlet is a complete packet and stays together.
  • Log the pamphlet number and your name when you take it and when you return it.
  • Keep completed sheets clean, dry and flat.
  • Do not photograph or post completed signature lines.
  • Return packets daily or when the Petition Captain says.

Affidavit basics (34 O.S. § 6)

  • You must be a registered Oklahoma voter, 18 or older.
  • You must personally witness every signature on the sheets you circulate.
  • Sign only your own affidavit — never a blank one, never another circulator's.
  • Notarize only after collection; first strike one line through each fully EMPTY row (never a filled one) — nothing is added after the oath.

If challenged or harassed

  • Stay calm. Do not argue. Say: “Signing only supports putting the issue to a public vote.”
  • Step away if anyone becomes aggressive. Call the Petition Captain.
  • Write down date, time, location, witnesses and what happened.
  • Return the packet if it may have been damaged or compromised.
